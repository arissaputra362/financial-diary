## 1. Product direction

FinDiary is a personal and shared household finance application. Its purpose is to help people understand, record, organize, and improve their financial habits.

The product should feel approachable to younger users without becoming childish. It should feel trustworthy enough for sensitive financial information without becoming visually cold or corporate.

## 2. Design objective

Create a mobile-first experience that is:
- Fast for everyday money recording.
- Clear when reviewing financial health.
- Useful for planning and monitoring budgets.
- Understandable when managing multiple wallets.
- Safe and transparent for shared household finances.
- Convenient for capturing receipt data.
- Clear when tracking assets such as gold.

## 3. Known feature areas

These are known areas from product-owner context and must be verified against the repository:
- Home / financial dashboard.
- Budget planning and monitoring.
- Income and expense activities.
- Receipt scanning and transaction draft review.
- Gold asset recording and portfolio overview.
- Wallet management, including personal and shared wallets.
- Shared finance, household/member collaboration, and related approval flows.

The agent must discover additional features, nested pages, and workflows.

## 4. Audience and usage assumptions

Primary usage is expected to be mobile and frequent, often for quick transaction entry and balance checks. Users may also use desktop for reviewing reports or managing data.

Do not assume every user has the same financial literacy. Use plain Indonesian language where the existing product language is Indonesian. Avoid jargon unless it is explained.

## 5. Product experience principles

1. **Clarity before decoration:** financial amounts and actions must be immediately understandable.
2. **Fast capture:** common transaction tasks should require minimal steps.
3. **Context matters:** personal and shared money must be visually and semantically distinguishable.
4. **Trust:** explain estimates, scanned data, and calculated values.
5. **Progress, not pressure:** encourage better habits without shaming users.
6. **Useful insights:** insights must be grounded in actual available data.
7. **Consistency:** the same action should behave consistently across the app.
8. **Accessibility:** do not rely on color alone to communicate financial meaning.

## 6. Brand personality

- Friendly, optimistic, calm.
- Smart but not intimidating.
- Youthful but not childish.
- Trustworthy and transparent.
- Encouraging rather than judgmental.

## 7. Scope boundaries

This revamp primarily targets UI/UX and frontend presentation. Backend or data-model changes are out of scope unless the product owner separately approves them.

Optional new product ideas must be listed separately in `docs/revamp/docs/audit/OPEN_QUESTIONS_AND_RISKS.md` or a product opportunities document. Do not mix speculative features into the core revamp.
