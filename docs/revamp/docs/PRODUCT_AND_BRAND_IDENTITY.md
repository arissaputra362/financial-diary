# FinDiary — Product & Brand Identity

## 1. Positioning

**FinDiary — Kelola keuangan, raih masa depan.**

A friendly, modern companion for personal and shared financial organization.

## 2. Brand traits

| Trait | Expression | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Friendly | Warm microcopy, approachable illustrations | Childish cartoon UI |
| Trustworthy | Clear labels, transparent calculations | Hype, exaggerated claims |
| Calm | Restrained palette, clean layouts | Loud gradients and clutter |
| Smart | Useful insights and clear visualization | Unexplained jargon |
| Youthful | Modern typography and subtle personality | Trend effects that age quickly |

## 3. Visual identity direction

- Primary brand family: forest/emerald green.
- Supporting family: sage and mint.
- Neutral surfaces: warm off-white in light mode and deep green-charcoal in dark mode.
- Shared finance may use a restrained secondary blue accent if this helps distinguish scope.
- Gold asset visualization may use gold as a semantic accent, not as a global brand color.

## 4. Finny mascot concept (provisional)

Finny is a proposed small sprout/leaf character symbolizing gradual financial growth. The name and final character design are not locked until approved.

### Intended use
- Onboarding and feature introduction.
- Empty states.
- Achievement or positive milestone moments.
- Occasional contextual tips.

### Do not use
- As a permanent decoration on every screen.
- Inside dense data tables or transaction rows.
- As a replacement for clear icons or labels.
- In error states where it could make serious issues feel trivial.
- In a way that competes with balances, totals, or primary actions.

### Asset requirements
If approved, create a consistent asset family:
- Primary mascot pose.
- Neutral/idle.
- Encouraging.
- Celebration.
- Empty-state pose.
- Light-mode and dark-mode variants where required.
- SVG or transparent raster assets at suitable resolutions.

Do not invent a new mascot appearance in every page. Until approved assets exist, use the approved logo, neutral line icons, or restrained abstract illustrations.

## 5. Logo and naming

Use existing production logo assets if present. Do not redraw or replace the logo without approval. Verify spelling, capitalization, and existing brand usage in the repository.

## 6. Tone of voice

Use concise, clear, supportive Indonesian:
- “Catat pengeluaran” rather than ambiguous labels.
- “Budget tersisa” rather than unexplained financial jargon.
- “Periksa hasil scan” to signal user review.
- “Belum ada transaksi” for empty states.

Avoid judgmental copy such as “Kamu boros”. Prefer neutral language such as “Pengeluaran kategori ini lebih tinggi dari bulan lalu”.

## 7. Brand approval gates

The following require product-owner approval:
- Final mascot name and artwork.
- Logo redesign.
- Major brand color changes.
- New product positioning or tagline.
- New gamification mechanics.
